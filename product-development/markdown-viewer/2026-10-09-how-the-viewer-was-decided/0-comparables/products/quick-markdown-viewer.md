## V37 Why the product exists
README_FOR_APES.md "Why it exists": "Most Markdown tools are either: - editors first, with reading as a side effect - browser-based, even when shipped as desktop apps - hard to test deterministically across Apple platforms. This project is trying to do the opposite." Developer page "Why it is different": "The renderer stays native. There is no WKWebView, HTML, CSS, or JavaScript rendering layer between you and your documents."
