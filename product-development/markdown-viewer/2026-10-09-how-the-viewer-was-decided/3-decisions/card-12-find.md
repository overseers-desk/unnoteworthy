**Cost lines:**
- Search within the document: exists, and reaches folded sections and table cells (capability note).
- Search across a folder: the program shows one document and does not open more than one (capability note, "What it does not do"); a folder search is built new and is not costed.
- Find and replace: needs an editing surface and saving, which do not exist (card 9's cost line).
- Regular expressions: how the find bar matches is not recorded in the capability note; the find bar is streamdoc's, the operator's own module, so a change lands upstream by the same hands.

**Opposite:** Why not no find at all, and why not the folder rather than the page? The largest reader by installs, glow (59,634 Homebrew installs a year, N1), claims no search, and 55 of the 86 reader rivals are silent on it, so take-up does not need it printed. The folder search answers a different question, which file a word is in, from the one this card asks, where the word is in the file I am reading.
