# The questions

Each line is a parameter this offering has, put in the words the buyer would use to ask about it. The buyer is the person who installs or pays, which on day one is open: the strike list beside this file is where the owner says who is out.

A card earns its place only where its ruling would change what the seller says at the point of sale. Where a question below turns out not to, it is clerical and goes to the design phase as freedom rather than to the owner.

## The questions the owner's words raise

The owner's words, verbatim: "the current name was a placeholder." and "not only if the product can be sold as is, but also what small changes can be made to make it sell."

**What is it called?** The owner says the present name is a placeholder, so the name is open: what do I call this when I tell a colleague, and what do I type when I go looking for it?

**What does "sold" mean here?** Is a sale money changing hands, an install, a star, a package pulled from a store, or a seat in an organisation? The owner asks whether it can be sold; the unit card and the price card are where that word gets its meaning, and the answer shapes every seller's sentence.

**Is this one thing or several?** Am I getting one program, or a program and the parts it is built from, or a family of related tools from the same maker? Card 0 as the method names it.

**What is a small change?** The owner asks what small changes would make it sell. That is the scoping ruling of the Game: which dimension the arms compete on. It is not a card; it is the owner's ruling that opens the Game, and it waits on the cards.

## The questions the method names

**Who is this for?** Who installs it, who pays for it where someone pays, and whose files does it open?

**Where do I get it?** Do I find it in a store, a package repository, a browser, a page of the maker's own, or a link someone sends me, and who am I dealing with when I do?

**What am I getting one of?** A download, a licence per person, a subscription, a package my system updates, a component I build into my own program, or support?

**When?** Does it matter when in the year I come looking, and does the product change on a cadence I should know about?

**What does it cost?** What do I pay, once or recurring, per person or per organisation, and what does it cost me beyond the price to use it?

## The questions this product's parameters raise

Ordered as a buyer meets them.

### Before installing

**Will it run on my machine?** Which operating systems, and what do I need installed first?

**How do I install it, and how do I keep it current?**

**Will it open my files?** Which flavour of markdown does it read, and what happens to the parts it does not understand?

**Can I write in it, or only read?**

**Does it need the network, and does anything leave my machine?**

**Can I use it at work?** Under what terms, and does my employer need a licence?

### While reading

**How do I find my way around a long document?**

**How do I find a word in it?**

**What happens when I click a link, an image, or a reference to another file?**

**Does it keep up with a file I am editing elsewhere?**

**What does it look like, and can I change that?**

**Can I print it, export it, or send it on?**

### After

**Where do I get help, and what happens when it breaks?**

**Who else uses it, and will it still exist next year?**
