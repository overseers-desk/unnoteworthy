## Notes on this list, which are not rows

**Why "not searched" fills the second column.** The rank database has no API units this month, and the method says a run with no keyword instrument records the gap rather than standing in for it with judgment. The located-capture instrument exists and was not spent on day one, since a capture reads one results page for one term and the terms are seeded after the buyer is ruled. Flathub's installs-last-month figures in the day-one pull are a demand count per app, not a search, and they sit with the comparables.

**How the rows were drawn.** From the audience phrases the 37 pages quote, grouped where the seller's sentence would be the same, with the published lists read for buyers the pages leave silent. The number of rows was not fixed in advance: it is the count of buyer classes those phrases name, plus two the pages leave silent that the operator's own records or modules reach (the AI-agent reader through questlog, the embedding developer through teatotal). Marp's and lookatme's presenter, who turns markdown into slides, is another category's buyer and is not a row; the owner can add it. Mobile users are a platform, not a buyer, and sit inside whichever row they belong to.

**The limit under every third-column count.** The 37 pages are a convenience list the orchestrator chose, read once through a summarising fetch tool, with READMEs truncated. A page silent on its audience sells to somebody; every "n of 37" is a floor. The frame, the frozen codebook and a coded variable for whom each product addresses come after the owner returns this list, with one source-list set per unstruck row.

**The limit under every fourth-column zero.** The offering has no surface, so its record is empty by construction, not by the market's verdict. The sibling records are for other products on other terms and are kept apart as the method asks.

**Sources named by this list.** `0-comparables/2026-10-09-day-one-pull.md` (the 37 pages and the three lists); `1-competitions/sales-record.md`; `1-competitions/capability-note.md`.
