### Boundary tests

**Opposite:** Why not a closed licence with a stated work-use term, as the paid market does (106 of 496 proprietary, mostly on AlternativeTo)? Among readers it is 1 of 86, and none of the 28 operator-shaped readers does it. A public repository reached by clone is already source made visible; a closed licence on it would tell the developer they may look but not use.

**Further:** Why not go further than open source, and dedicate it to the public domain, or add an explicit commercial-use grant on the page? The public domain is 1 of 496 (taxonomy V16(c)), and an explicit "free for work use" sentence is 2 of 204 in SHAPE. No figure carries either past the licence.
