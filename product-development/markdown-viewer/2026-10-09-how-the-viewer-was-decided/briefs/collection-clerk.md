# Collection clerk: verbatim profiles

Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, then `briefs/venue-situation.md`, then `/usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/sage-S-survey.md` section 1 step 6, then `0-comparables/codebook-v1.md`, then `0-comparables/frame-review.md`, then `0-comparables/frame/frame.tsv`. Nothing else in the repository is open to you. The launch message names your shard: the products of `frame.tsv` assigned to you, by name.

For each product in your shard, read what it publishes: the listing it was drawn from (the identifier in the frame) and, where that listing links one, the product's own site or repository README, and from there its pricing, download or features page where linked. Obey the frame review's condition on the product's cell. Write one file per product, `0-comparables/products/<slug>.md`, the slug being the product name in lower case with hyphens, holding:

- the product's name or names as published, and every URL read with the date and the HTTP status or error;
- for each variable in the codebook, under a heading naming the variable, the exact text the product publishes on that subject, quoted, with the URL it came from; where the pages say nothing on the subject, the single word "silent";
- nothing else. No coding, no inference, no summary, no judgement of the product.

A page that cannot be reached is recorded as unreachable with the error, and no other source stands in for it. Where a listing and the product's own site disagree, quote both with their URLs. Keep each profile under about two hundred lines by quoting the relevant passages rather than whole pages.

Your final message gives the count of profiles written against the shard's size, the products with any page unreachable, and what surprised you.
