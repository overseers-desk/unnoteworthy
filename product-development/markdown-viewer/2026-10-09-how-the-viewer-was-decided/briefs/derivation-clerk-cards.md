The second owner gate is one ruling round: the buyer card and every card that follows it. `3-decisions/leading-buyers.md` holds the seller's sentence for each leading buyer and the fourteen parameters those sentences contain; the fourteenth is card 0, already written. The other thirteen are cards 2 to 14, numbered in that list's order, and three clerks write them by area. The launch message names your area.

Write each card on `forms/card.md`, every field the form names except the Priors and Held lines, which belong to another clerk, with the Ruled line reading "open". The question is the parameter's question from the list, in the words a buyer meets it. The options are the values the market shows, each with its figure in words and population, its source, its capability leg from the capability note (a value the program does not deliver today is an option all the same, with its cost line, since a capability not yet built is a cost and not a bound), and a seller's sentence to a roster profile named by row and the roster's own description, never by name. The owner asked "not only if the product can be sold as is, but also what small changes can be made to make it sell": the program as it stands is one option on every card where the market shows it, and the changes are the others, each with what it costs to build.

Per buyer. The leading buyers are three: developers and writers together, the documentation reader (RULED strike-list:developers-and-coders, RULED strike-list:writers-and-authors), and developers and coders without writers (RULED strike-list:developers-and-coders), the two options card 1 withholds between; and readers of what AI agents write (RULED strike-list:readers-of-what-ai-agents-write), whom the market-only reading names beside the documentation reader. The owner rules the buyer in the same round as these cards, so where a card's options or recommendation differ by buyer, the card shows each: the Recommended line gives the recommendation under the documentation reader, the one buyer both readings of card 1 name, and the Joint line opens "Decided with card 1." and gives, buyer by buyer, the option the evidence carries under each other leading buyer with its margin, or says the card does not differ by buyer. The findings by buyer at the end of `0-comparables/findings.md` are the per-buyer figures; where they do not separate a buyer from a cell, the Joint line says so.

Card 2, the name, carries the candidate names priced against each other: volume and difficulty for each (the rank database has no units this month, so each reads "not searched"), and the collision verdict for each, from a located results-page capture of the candidate name, through the serpapi skill if available, recorded with its date and place. Its options are candidate names drawn from the ways the market names such products (finding 25 and the search findings), each figured by the prevalence of its naming pattern and its collision verdict. Card 3, where it is had, cannot be ruled without a search-demand leg; with no volume this month, its Recommended line is withheld or carries the open fact, as the method's two-legs section says, and names the observation that would close it. Card 4 carries the market's own unit words as the weaker evidence it is. The price card takes no search leg.

On every card the Recommended line follows the form to the letter: the margin in the runner-up's unit, what it rests on, the instrument that saw both options and its lean, withheld where one comparable coded the other way would erase the lead. The figure column and the Recommended line reach the owner as written, so they state the population in words; codes go in the source column. The four boundary tests are argued between the options. Where the evidence shows fewer than two figured options, the card is a named gap for Survey and says which record would close it. When your cards are written, run `python3 /usr/local/src/aesop/pmf-sage/tools/card-check.py .` from the run folder and clear every refusal it prints for your cards; the refusals for cards outside your area are another clerk's.

Your final message lists, per card, the options with their figures, the Recommended line, the per-buyer readings, what the card check printed for your cards, and anything the evidence could not give you.
