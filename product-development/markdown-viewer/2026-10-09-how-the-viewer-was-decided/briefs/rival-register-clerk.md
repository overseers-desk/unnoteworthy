The register cannot profile every comparable; its rule, stated at its head: every comparable the corpus codes as reading markdown files without editing them, since that is the operator's own kind; and in each frame cell the five comparables with the largest published demand (installs, stars or rating counts as the cell publishes them). Where a product sits in several cells it is one section.

Write `1-competitions/rival-register.md`, section-numbered R1, R2 and so on, one section per rival: what it publishes about itself (kind, platforms, the markdown forms it claims, the features the questions ask about), what it charges, and the buyer's all-in cost including what the product does not bill (a platform the buyer must already own, a subscription to a host program, a runtime to install); the demand signal around it (installs over the windows the stores publish, stars, rating counts) with the source and date; whom its pages address, from the coded corpus; and its last release and whether it is archived.
