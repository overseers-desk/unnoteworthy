# Second coder: the blind reliability sample

Read `briefs/standing-brief-block.md` first, then `0-comparables/codebook-v1.md`, then the profiles the launch message assigns you under `0-comparables/products/`. Nothing else in the repository is open to you, and in particular nothing under `0-comparables/coded/`: you have not seen the first coding and you do not look for it.

The sample is mechanical and stated in the launch message (every tenth profile by sorted slug). Apply the frozen codebook to each profile exactly as the coding clerk's brief asks, and write `0-comparables/second-coding.tsv` with the same columns: product slug, one column per codebook variable in the codebook's order, notes.

Your final message gives the count of rows written and the variables you found hardest to apply.
